Look at what this task taught us and propose, for my approval, additions to:

- `.claude/knowledge/BUSINESS_RULES.md`: a rule the business confirmed (Confirmed) or we assumed (Uncertain).
- `.claude/knowledge/KNOWN_PITFALLS.md`: a gotcha that can happen again (symptom, cause, fix).
- `<decisions folder>/ADR-00X-title.md`: a non-trivial decision (format in that folder's `README.md`). The decisions folder is `paths.decisions_dir` of the flow config (`.flow/flow.toml`, else `ops/flow.toml`).

Add only what is new and reusable; no issue-specific detail, no duplicates. Show the text and write it after I approve.
