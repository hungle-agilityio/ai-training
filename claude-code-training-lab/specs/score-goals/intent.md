# Intent: score-goals

## Problem

A business owner sees each location's score but has no way to say what score a location should reach. Staff cannot tell at a glance which locations are on target and how far the others are behind.

## Goal

Each location can have a target score. The dashboard and the location page show whether the location meets it, or how many points it is short.

## Users

The owner and staff of a business with several locations, signed in to ReviewRadar. Everyone who is signed in can see and set goals (the app has no roles).

## Out of scope

- A history of goals, or who changed them.
- Goals on the monthly report, the Compare page, the CSV exports and the dashboard API.
- Roles or permissions.
